Privacy & terms

The short version: Mizavo keeps only what the game needs to work and keep score. No ads, no selling data, no outside trackers, no cookies.

If you log in with Twitch

Twitch tells Mizavo your Twitch username, display name, account ID and profile picture. Twitch also shares the email address on your Twitch account. It's kept by our login system, and Mizavo never uses it to contact you or sends it anywhere else.

For your channel, Mizavo keeps your game settings, your games (questions shown, your picks, how chat voted, scores), which questions your channel has already seen so they never repeat, and any question reports you send.

Your channel's record and your chat's top scores appear on a public channel page and on the leaderboards, unless you hide your channel.

If you answer in a streamer's chat

Viewers never log in to Mizavo. When you type an answer in the chat of a channel playing Mizavo, the game records your Twitch username, display name, Twitch user ID and your answers, so you can earn points on that channel's leaderboard. Mizavo reads only answers to the game, not the rest of chat.

If the streamer has chat pictures turned on, your public Twitch profile picture is shown on your ball and saved for a short time so it loads quickly.

If you play the daily challenge

Logged in, your answers, score and streaks are saved and your display name can appear on the daily leaderboard. As a guest, nothing about you is saved.

Family logins are made by Mizavo's owner for his own family members, with a parent's OK. They store only a username, a display name and game scores.

Daily reminders

If you tap “Remind me” on the daily challenge page and allow notifications, Mizavo saves the push address your browser makes for it, your time zone, and (if you're logged in) your account, so it can send one reminder at 9 am your time when a new challenge is live, and skip it if you've already played. Turn it off any time with “Turn off” on that page, or in your phone's notification settings.

Visit counts

Mizavo counts its own visits so it can tell what's working. For each page view it saves: the page, the site that linked you here (just its name), phone/tablet/desktop, browser name, time zone, language and a random ID made in your browser.

No names, no IP addresses, no cookies, and no outside analytics company.

Your browser also remembers a few small things in local storage (not cookies): that you're logged in, your sound volume, your chat name and that random visit ID. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Services Mizavo runs on

  • Twitch: login, reading chat answers, profile pictures.
  • Supabase: the database and login system. It keeps standard security logs of logins.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website. Like any web host, it handles your connection to load the pages.
  • Google Fonts: the site's fonts load from Google.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla push services: deliver reminders, only if you turn them on.
  • Ko-fi: only if you click a Support link. Tips happen on Ko-fi's site.

Mizavo doesn't sell or rent any data, and doesn't show ads.

Your controls

  • Hide your channel: on the game screen, open More options → Your channel and turn off Public channel. Your channel page and leaderboard spot are hidden.
  • Delete your data: email us from anywhere and say which Twitch name it's about. We'll delete your channel or viewer data, or tell you what we have, within 30 days.
  • Log out any time from the game screen.

Data is kept while Mizavo is running, or until you ask us to delete it.

Terms

  • Free to play. Mizavo is free and may change, pause or end at any time. Features and points may change while it's in testing.
  • Play fair. Don't cheat, use bots, spam answers, or try to break or overload the site. We can remove scores or block channels that do.
  • Questions. Every question is checked against a source, but mistakes happen. Questions can be fixed or removed, and that can change scores. Use “Report this question” if something's wrong.
  • On stream. Streamers are responsible for their own stream and Twitch's rules. Mizavo isn't made by or connected with Twitch.
  • No guarantees. Mizavo is provided as it is, without warranties.

If these change, the date at the top changes too.
