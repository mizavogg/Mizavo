You vs your chat,
in five minutes.

Mizavo is a trivia game where you play against your own Twitch chat. Every vote in chat drops into its answer as a dot, live on screen. You pick a side, chat picks theirs, and the wrong pile pops. Thanks for helping test it.

1. Log in 2. Pick your settings 3. Put it on stream 4. Play 5. Report a bad question Fixes Feedback

1Log in with Twitch

Open the game page and press Log in with Twitch. Mizavo uses the login only to know which channel is yours, so your games and scores stay with it. It reads your chat anonymously, never posts in it, and can't change anything on your account.

Mizavo is invite-only while we test. If you see "not on the invite list yet", send your Twitch name to whoever invited you, then press Check again.

2Pick your settings

Mode
Fact or Fake: one statement, is it true? Odd One Out: four things, which doesn't belong? Mix: both, shuffled.
Twitch chat to read
Your channel name. It's filled in for you. Change it only if you want to read a different chat.
Rounds & seconds
10 rounds of 15 seconds is about 5 minutes. Use 20 or 30 seconds if your stream has a delay, so chat sees the question in time.
Blind mode
Chat's votes stay mixed together until you lock in your answer, so you can't just copy chat. Recommended.
Simulated chat
Fake demo viewers for practice. Turn it off for real games on stream. With it on, the game is marked as a test and doesn't count.
Streamer mode
Clears space on the right for your webcam and chat overlay. Drag the edge or use the slider to size it. The game keeps at least 720px.

3Put it on stream (OBS)

  1. Open Mizavo in its own browser window (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) and log in there.
  2. Make the window at least 1280 × 720. Full screen on a second monitor works best.
  3. In OBS, add a source: + → Window Capture, and pick the Mizavo browser window. On macOS, use macOS Screen Capture set to that window.
  4. Crop off the browser's tabs and address bar: hold Alt (Option on Mac) and drag the top edge of the source in OBS.
  5. Using streamer mode? Line up your webcam and chat sources inside the dashed box. During the game that space stays empty and matches the background.

Why not a Browser Source? It needs the Twitch login, and OBS's built-in browser makes that awkward. A captured window keeps you logged in and lets you click and use keys normally.

4Play

What chat types

Fact or Fake: 1 or 2 (or fact / fake).

Odd One Out: A B C D (or 1–4).

Each viewer's latest vote counts, so they can change their mind until time runs out.

What you press

Pick with the number keys, the arrow keys, A–D, or by clicking.

Enter reveals early once you've picked, and moves to the next round.

End game is top right. A game you close by accident waits for you to resume.

You score a point for each right answer, and chat scores a point when its most popular answer is right. At the end you'll see the final score and your top chatters.

A question never repeats on your channel, so play as much as you like.

5Report a bad question

Every question is fact-checked with a source, but mistakes happen. After a round, press Report this question under the explanation and pick what's wrong: a wrong answer, out of date, unclear, too local (not known worldwide), offensive, or a typo. Reports go straight to us, and bad questions are taken out of the game.

Fixes

No votes show up
Check the pill next to the logo shows your channel (like #yourname) with a green dot, and that the chat name in settings matches your channel. Votes must be a message on their own, like 2.
"Not enough new questions"
Try fewer rounds or another mode. Odd One Out has fewer questions during the test.
The page reloaded mid-game
Log back in. You'll be offered Resume for the game you were playing.
Stream delay
Use longer rounds (20–30 seconds) so chat sees each question before time runs out.
